My name is Antony Carrard, I provide bioimage analysis support for the [Faculty of Biology and Medicine (FBM)](https://www.unil.ch/fbm/en/home.html) at the [University of Lausanne (UNIL)](https://www.unil.ch/unil/en/home.html). I help researchers extract numbers from their data, which ranges from microscopic images to animal behavior video analysis. I also provide support with image acquisition and hardware selection. You can find out more about my role on my official [support page](https://wiki.unil.ch/ci/books/research-support/page/bioimage-analysis).

I hold a bachelor's in electrical engineering and a master's in data science, with a thesis on extracting bone surfaces from ultrasound images at [UniGe](https://www.unige.ch/). I then worked as a software developer in the automation industry, building custom solutions for vision quality control. I joined UNIL to explore further research projects in the biomedical field.
